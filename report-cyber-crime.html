INDIA / OFFICIAL REPORTING ROUTES

Report cybercrime.
Use official channels.

CWO can provide awareness and general guidance, but is not a substitute for law enforcement or an official reporting portal.

Open National Cyber Crime Portal ↗Call 1930 for financial cyber fraud
STEP_01

Preserve evidence

Keep relevant URLs, messages, transaction IDs, screenshots, timestamps, and complaint/reference numbers. Avoid editing original files where possible.

STEP_02

Contact your bank quickly

For suspected financial fraud, contact your bank/payment provider immediately and use official fraud-reporting channels.

STEP_03

Submit an official report

Use the Government of India's National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal. Do not share passwords, OTPs, or full payment credentials with people offering unofficial help.

Official portal: cybercrime.gov.in. Helpline 1930 is used for reporting financial cyber fraud in India. For immediate physical danger, contact local emergency services.
