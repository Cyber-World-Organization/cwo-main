FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear answers.
Safer choices.

What is Cyber World Organisation?

CWO focuses on cyber awareness, responsible security education, digital forensics learning, community programmes, and software/AI development.

Are all listed courses currently available?

The course pages provide topic outlines. Contact CWO to confirm which programmes are active, plus dates, format, instructors, prerequisites, and fees.

Can beginners join the N00B Hack3r Community?

The community is presented as beginner-friendly. Ethical hacking and bug bounty practice must remain within explicitly authorised scopes and controlled labs.

How do I report cybercrime in India?

Visit the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal at cybercrime.gov.in. For financial cyber fraud, call 1930 promptly. If there is immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Does listing an organisation as an engagement mean formal partnership?

No. A logo, name, or link alone does not establish endorsement or formal partnership. Confirm any relationship through official documentation.

How can I volunteer or request a course?

Use the Volunteer or Contact Us page to send an enquiry. A working submission backend must be configured before online form submissions can be received.
